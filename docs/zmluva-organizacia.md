> [zmluva o spracúvaní údajov podľa čl. 28](gdpr-zmluva-cl28.md)** — a to na
> šiestich miestach (§1.1, §1.3, §1.4, §7.3, §11.4, príloha A). Do 2026-08-30

> druhá**.

### 1. Subject matter

**1.1** The Provider gives the Organisation access to the **P.L.A.W**

**1.2** The service comprises coach accounts with a roster of assigned players,

disciplines stated in Annex 1.

**1.3** The service is provided in the form in which it exists at the time of
use. The Provider develops it continuously; section 12 governs changes.

**1.4** The processing of personal data is governed by a separate **data
processing agreement** under Article 28 of the GDPR, which forms an inseparable
part of this agreement. **Where the two differ on the processing of personal
data, the data processing agreement prevails.**

**1.5** The Organisation does not enter into this agreement as a consumer.

### 2. Seats

**2.1** A **seat** is one coach account with active membership of the
Organisation.

**2.2** The head coach (director) **does not occupy a seat.**

**2.3** The number of seats is stated in Annex 1. **The minimum is three seats.**

**2.4** The application enforces the number of seats: connecting a further coach
beyond the agreed number is refused.

**2.5** Further seats may be ordered in writing (email suffices) and are invoiced
pro rata to the end of the current period.

**2.6** **A seat freed during a period is not refunded** — a seat is capacity for
the period, not a specific person. The Organisation may replace the coach
occupying it at any time.

### 3. Price, invoicing and payment

**3.1** The price is **49 EUR per seat per month, exclusive of VAT.**

**3.2** The Organisation states in Annex 1 whether it is invoiced **monthly** or
**annually in advance**. The choice does not affect the term under section 4.

**3.3** Annual payment in advance for the whole period attracts a discount
according to the number of seats:

| Seats | Discount | Price per seat per year excl. VAT |
|---|---|---|
| 3–5 | 15 % | 499.80 EUR |
| 6–11 | 20 % | 470.40 EUR |
| 12 and more | 30 % | 411.60 EUR |

**3.4** The number of seats decisive for the discount is the number agreed in
Annex 1 at the start of the period. Seats ordered later under section 2.5 are
invoiced at the same discount pro rata. **A discount is not recalculated
retroactively**; if the number of seats crosses into a higher band, the higher
discount applies from the next period.

**3.5** Monthly invoices are issued at the beginning of each month, annual
invoices at the beginning of the period.

**3.6** Invoices are payable within **14 days** of issue.

**3.7** In the event of late payment the Provider is entitled to statutory
interest. If payment is more than **30 days** overdue and a written reminder has
been sent, the Provider may suspend access until payment. **Suspension does not
delete data.**

**3.8** The Provider may change the price with effect from the next period,
provided it announces the change **at least 60 days** before the end of the
current period. The Organisation may then terminate under section 4.2.

### 4. Term, renewal and termination

**4.1** The agreement is concluded for **12 months** from the start date in
Annex 1.

**4.2** It renews automatically for a further 12 months unless either Party gives
written notice **at least 30 days before the end** of the current period.

**4.3** Either Party may terminate with immediate effect where the other Party
materially breaches the agreement and fails to remedy the breach within 30 days
of a written notice.

**4.4** On early termination by the Organisation, fees already paid are not
refunded. **This does not apply** where the Organisation terminates under
section 4.3 for the Provider's breach, under section 12.2, or under section 6.3
of the data processing agreement (objection to a subprocessor) — in those cases
the Provider refunds the fees pro rata.

### 5. Support

**5.1** Support is provided by email at the Provider's contact address, in Slovak
or English, on business days.

**5.2** The Provider **aims** to respond within one business day. **This is a
target, not a guaranteed response time.**

**5.3** Support covers use of the service and the correction of faults. Work
requested beyond that is charged at the rates in **Annex 2**.

### 6. Availability and maintenance

**6.1** The Provider operates the service with reasonable professional care but
**does not guarantee any particular level of availability.** No uptime percentage
is agreed.

**6.2** Planned maintenance involving downtime is announced by email **at least
3 days in advance** and is carried out outside 08:00–20:00 CET where possible.

**6.3** Outages at the infrastructure providers listed in Annex B of the data
processing agreement are outside the Provider's control. The Provider informs the
Organisation of such outages of which it becomes aware.

**6.4** Section 6.1 concerns the accessibility of the application. **It does not
limit the Provider's obligation to protect the data itself against loss**, which
is governed by the security measures under the data processing agreement.

### 7. The Organisation's data

**7.1** All data entered into the service by the Organisation and its coaches
**belongs to the Organisation.**

**7.2** **The Provider does nothing of its own with that data:** no statistics
beyond the Organisation, no training of models, no benchmarking against other
organisations, no sale and no advertising. This mirrors section 2.3 of the data
processing agreement.

**7.3** The Organisation may request an export of its data at any time. Where the
service does not yet offer an export function, the Provider prepares it; the work
is charged under **Annex 2**, save for the assistance provided free of charge
under section 7.3 of the data processing agreement.

**7.4** The return or deletion of data at the end of the service is governed by
section 9 of the data processing agreement.

### 8. The Organisation's obligations

**8.1** The Organisation provides accurate details in Annex 1 and **keeps its
contact persons up to date**, including the contact for notification of a
personal data breach under Annex A of the data processing agreement.

**8.2** **The Organisation informs its coaches that the head coach can see their

**8.3** The Organisation is responsible for having a legal basis for entering the

**8.4** The Organisation keeps access credentials secure and reports suspected

**8.5** The Organisation uses the service for its own training operation only. It

**8.6** The Organisation does not attempt to circumvent access controls. Security

## Poznámky k návrhu (nezverejňuje sa)

### Šesť rozhodnutí a prečo takto

1. **Sedadlo za 49 EUR mesačne bez DPH, najmenej tri (§2, §3.1).** Cenu určil
   user 2026-08-30. Trojka nie je náhodná — v [cenníku](cennik-navrh.md) §2 je
   hranica medzi produktmi postavená na **počte trénerov**, nie hráčov: „klub
   s tromi trénermi patrí do federačného, aj keby mal hráčov menej". Bez minima
   by sa federačný produkt začal predávať jednotlivcom a zožral by spotrebiteľský
   cenník.
   - **Zľava za ročnú platbu je ODSTUPŇOVANÁ podľa sedadiel** (§3.3): 3–5 → 15 %,
     6–11 → 20 %, 12+ → 30 %. User si vybral stupne namiesto pevných 30 % preto,
     aby veta **„až 30 %" bola pravdivá** — pri pevnej zľave by to bolo „−30 %".
     Vedľajší efekt, ktorý sa hodí: väčší zväz má dôvod brať viac sedadiel.
   - **§3.4 rieši otázku, ktorá by inak prišla pri prvom dokupovaní sedadiel:**
     zľava sa spätne neprepočítava, vyššie pásmo platí od nasledujúceho obdobia.
     Bez tejto vety by zväz s piatimi sedadlami dokúpil šieste a pýtal si vrátiť
     rozdiel za celý rok.
   - Spotrebiteľský produkt má pri ročnej platbe **−40 %**, teda viac než
     federačný. Nie je to nedopatrenie: tréner platí sám za seba a jedna hladina,
     kým zväz platí za sedadlá a dostáva riadiaci pult.
2. **12 mesiacov s automatickým obnovením a výpoveďou 30 dní vopred (§4).**
   **Mesačná fakturácia neznamená mesačnú viazanosť** (§3.2) — je to rok platený
   po častiach. Rozhodol tak user; ročná zľava má vďaka tomu o čo sa oprieť
   (rovnaký záväzok, iný spôsob platby) a sedadlá sa aj tak onboardujú ručne cez
   subdoménu a DNS, takže mesačný odchod by bol práca navyše.
   **§3.8 dáva na oznámenie zmeny ceny 60 dní** práve preto, aby organizácii
   ostalo 30 dní na výpoveď — inak by bolo právo vypovedať pri zdražení iluzórne.
3. **Žiadne percento dostupnosti (§6.1).** Jeden človek, bez pohotovosti a bez
   sledovania — 99,5 % by sme nevedeli ani odmerať. **§6.4 je dôležitejší, než
   vyzerá:** oddeľuje „appka je nedostupná" od „údaje sme stratili". To druhé je
   povinnosť podľa čl. 32 a tá sa neobmedzuje.
4. **Strop = odplata za posledných 12 mesiacov (§9.2).** Na túto klauzulu
   výslovne odkazuje §11.4 zmluvy podľa čl. 28 — dovtedy odkazovala do prázdna.
   Výnimky v §9.4 tam musia ostať, bez nich by bol strop v spore napadnuteľný.
5. **129 EUR bez DPH za každú začatú hodinu (príloha 2).** Sadzbu určil user.
   Pôvodne som navrhol účtovanie po polhodinách; **user to zamietol ako zbytočnú
   komplikáciu** — celé hodiny sa ľahšie fakturujú aj vysvetľujú.
   **Poistkou proti námietke, že je to prekážka plnenia povinností
   prevádzkovateľa, sú dve veci, ktoré už v zmluve o údajoch sú:** prvé 2 hodiny
   ročne zadarmo (§7.3) a to, že **pomoc sa nikdy nepodmieňuje platbou** (§7.4)
   — najprv sa spraví, potom fakturuje.
6. **Uvoľnené sedadlo sa nepreplácia (§2.6).** Sedadlo je kapacita, nie osoba —
   sedí to s tým, ako appka funguje: členstvo sa odoberie a sedadlo sa uvoľní pre
   iného. Zväz tým nič nestráca, len si musí nájsť náhradu.

### Vecné veci, ktoré si treba všimnúť

12. **§7.3 priznáva, že export ako funkcia neexistuje.** Je to zámerné —
    sľubovať tlačidlo, ktoré tam nie je, by bola prvá vec, na ktorej by nás
    zväz chytil. Keď funkcia pribudne, veta sa skráti.
13. **§8.2 presúva informačnú povinnosť voči trénerom na zväz.** To isté hovoria
    [zásady pre organizáciu](gdpr-zasady-organizacia.md) §5 ako informáciu — tu
    je to **povinnosť**. Bez toho by prvý spor trénera so zamestnávateľom
    o sledovaní skončil u nás.
14. **§10.4 je obrátený oproti bežným SaaS zmluvám** — zvyčajne si dodávateľ
    právo uvádzať zákazníka berie, my ho vyslovene nemáme. Pri zväzoch je to
    predajný argument, nie ústupok.
15. **§8.6 nezakazuje bezpečnostné testovanie, len ho viaže na dohodu.** Veľký
    zväz si ho vypýta a odmietnuť by pôsobilo zle — máme čím doložiť dva audity.
